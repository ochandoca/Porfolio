Find your way through a puzzle-filled manor while barely dodging human eating monsters. You wake up in a dark room with one main objective: escape those sharpened teeth. However you shall not worry, you will meet friends along the way.

Camera System

One of my main tasks within the project was to design and develop an advanced system of fixed and semi-fixed cameras that provides an immersive and dynamic visual experience. This system was implemented to suit the narrative and gameplay needs, allowing precise control over perspective and framing in each scenario of the game.

In addition, I developed a complementary system for cinematics, focused on highlighting dramatic moments in the game, such as the deaths of the main character or bosses. This system allowed the playback of predefined sequences that integrate smooth transitions, zooms and dramatic angles, ensuring that each key scene was impactful and memorable.

Going into more detail on the camera system. A system has been designed to control where the cameras go by means of empty points in the world. These points are related to a collision volume that represents the room you are in. Once you enter one of these volumes the camera transitions according to defined parameters.
