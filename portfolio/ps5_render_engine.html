PS5 Game Engine

Resume

This game engine has been developed over a period of six months, in conjunction with the DevKit for the PlayStation 5 console, together with a team of 4 programmers, an artist and a level designer. In addition to this, a demo game has been developed to test the game's capabilities. Rendering, physics and audio have been used to develop a video game.

Showcase

Work Carried Out

Mainly focused on the rendering system of the engine, which has PBR, lights and also shadows. In addition to debug systems such as line drawing and 3D text (bilboards) and 2D.

Renderer Features:

  • Mesh rendering using instancing and bindless resources such as textures.
  • Grass system, with steps of the players and wind
  • Deffered rendering with depth prepass
  • Full PBR lighting with shadows
  • Skeletal Animations Rendering
  • SkySphere
  • Text 2D and 3D (bilboard)
